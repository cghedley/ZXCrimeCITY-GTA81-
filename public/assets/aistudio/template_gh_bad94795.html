CRIME CITY

A ZX81 game, running as the real Z80 machine code. 32×24 characters, black and white, no sound, a 40-key keyboard.

How to play

Steer with 5 6 7 8 (left, down, up, right) or the arrow keys. 0 stops, E leaves your car. Walk into a traffic car (H) to steal it. Run over pedestrians (I) for cash, but the police (inverse Z) will chase you. On foot, they bust you. In a car, they dent it.

Drive to the $ to pick up a fare, then to the X before the timer runs out. Three lives. At the title screen press Q to drop back to BASIC.

When you beat the fifth score you type three initials with the keyboard. The game then offers a button that opens a ready-filled GitHub issue; press its green submit button (you need a free GitHub account). A minute or two later your score appears on the table everyone sees.

Scores are checked by a GitHub Action when they are submitted, but they come from your browser, so the table runs on trust. Until your score is accepted it only shows on your own device.
